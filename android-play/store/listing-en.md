It's 3 a.m. You have work in the morning, and the cat has decided it's the perfect time to yell. Get it to sleep before the alarm goes off.

Throw slippers, pillows, towels and anything else within reach. Things fly in an arc, so aim with a little lead. Stun the cat to collect your stuff back. An alert cat won't hand it over, it bites. Watch for a butt wiggle: that means a pounce is coming. Hit it in time and the jump falls apart.
